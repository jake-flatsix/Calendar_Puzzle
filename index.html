How to Play:

  • Select a date to reveal target squares
  • Left-click a piece to select it
  • Right-click on the board to cycle through orientations
  • Hover to preview placement
  • Left-click to place the piece
  • Click a placed piece to grab and reposition it
  • Click outside the board to remove the grabbed piece
  • Use Hint or Solve buttons for help!

How to Play:

  • Select a date to reveal target squares
  • Click a piece in the sidebar to rotate it
  • Click and hold to drag a piece onto the board
  • Release to drop the piece
  • Click a placed piece to rotate it in place
  • Click and hold a placed piece to move it
  • Drag off the board to remove a piece
  • Use Hint or Solve buttons for help!
